Every Detail
Engineered

Full-Wall Mirrors

Frameless, floor-to-ceiling. LED-backlit. Zero distortions. Positioned for form checks and aesthetic feedback at every angle.

Hygiene Protocol

Wipe stations every 8 meters. Constant staff patrol. No sweat residue, no odor. Clean floors, pristine lockers — always.

Air Architecture

Precision HVAC zoning. Fresh air circulation. Balanced temperature. Signature woody-citrus scent diffused subtly throughout.

Sound Design

Audiophile-grade ceiling speakers. Calibrated per zone. Curated playlists — peak energy during prime time, calm in the morning.

Digital locking system. Bench seating. Dry floors. Clean showers with consistent pressure. Hair dryers, toiletries, premium fixtures.

CPR-trained staff at all times. First aid stations. Emergency protocols. Spotter zones marked. Zero broken equipment policy.

Filtered water stations every zone. Chalk availability. Grip accessories. Anti-slip flooring throughout. Towel culture enforced.
